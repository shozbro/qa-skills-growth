# Practical prompts for soft and process skills

These skills do not suit multiple choice. Pick one prompt at random, write your answer in `tests/answers/<skill>-<date>.md`, and compare it with the pass criteria.

## L2: Test estimations

1. [beginner] List the steps you took on your last ticket and the time each took. Which step surprised you?
2. [intermediate] Pick any ticket you tested this week. Write the estimate you would have given at the start and compare with the actual time. Explain the gap in two lines.
3. [intermediate] A new CMS entity needs UI, API and forward-sync coverage. List the work items you would size and why each could grow.
4. [intermediate] Break a 'test the new webhook' task into pieces and give an optimistic, likely and pessimistic estimate for each.
5. [advanced] A teammate asks for a one-day estimate on a task with unknown dependencies. Write your reply.
6. [advanced] Your estimates are consistently 30% low. Describe how you would find out why and what you would change.

**Pass: names the work items, states assumptions and risks, gives a range, and does not hide uncertainty.**

## L7: Leadership

1. [beginner] Write down three things you could teach a new joiner in 15 minutes.
2. [intermediate] Describe something you owned end to end in the last quarter: goal, decisions you made, what you delegated, result.
3. [intermediate] Prepare the outline for a 20-minute KT session you would give the team. Who is the audience and what should they be able to do afterwards?
4. [intermediate] Two teammates disagree about the test approach. How do you help them reach a decision?
5. [advanced] A teammate keeps missing handoffs. Write the conversation you would have.
6. [advanced] You must hand over the BTS automation to another engineer in two weeks. Write the handover plan and how you will know it worked.

**Pass: clear ownership, concrete decisions, a measurable outcome, and a respectful, specific message.**

## L8: Problem solving, creativity, curiosity, innovation

1. [beginner] Name one repetitive task you do weekly and how you could shorten it.
2. [intermediate] Describe a recurring QA pain point. Write it as problem, two options, your pick and the result.
3. [intermediate] Sketch a small tool that would save your team an hour a week. What does it do and how would you check it works?
4. [intermediate] Pick a bug that took long to find. What would have found it sooner?
5. [advanced] Pick something you do not understand in the BTS or CMS flow. Write three questions and how you would answer them.
6. [advanced] Propose an improvement to the team's test process. Include the problem, evidence, options, cost and how you would measure success.

**Pass: the problem is specific, options are compared, a decision is justified, and there is a way to check the result.**
