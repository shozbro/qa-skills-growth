# Practical prompts for soft and process skills

These skills do not suit multiple choice. Pick one prompt at random, write your answer in `tests/answers/<skill>-<date>.md`, and compare it with the pass criteria.

## L2: Test estimations

1. Pick any ticket you tested this week. Write the estimate you would have given at the start and compare with the actual time. Explain the gap in two lines.
2. A new CMS entity needs UI, API and forward-sync coverage. List the work items you would size and why each could grow.
3. A teammate asks for a one-day estimate on a task with unknown dependencies. Write your reply.

**Pass: names the work items, states assumptions and risks, gives a range, and does not hide uncertainty.**

## L7: Leadership

1. Describe something you owned end to end in the last quarter: goal, decisions you made, what you delegated, result.
2. Prepare the outline for a 20-minute KT session you would give the team. Who is the audience and what should they be able to do afterwards?
3. A teammate keeps missing handoffs. Write the conversation you would have.

**Pass: clear ownership, concrete decisions, a measurable outcome, and a respectful, specific message.**

## L8: Problem solving, creativity, curiosity, innovation

1. Describe a recurring QA pain point. Write it as problem, two options, your pick and the result.
2. Sketch a small tool that would save your team an hour a week. What does it do and how would you check it works?
3. Pick something you do not understand in the BTS or CMS flow. Write three questions and how you would answer them.

**Pass: the problem is specific, options are compared, a decision is justified, and there is a way to check the result.**
